Smart Hisab 360 – Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Smart Hisab 360 home

Smart Hisab 360 is a personal money tracker for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your records stay on your phone, and in your own Google Drive only if you choose to sign in. We don't run a server, we can't see your data, and the app has no ads or tracking.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The entries you add: expenses, income, transfers, accounts (including people you lend to or borrow from), categories, budgets, bills, savings goals and notes.
  • Your profile: the name and photo you choose. The photo is picked from your gallery and stored inside the app.
  • App settings such as theme, currency, reminders and backup time.
  • Backup files that the app makes, saved in the app's own storage on your phone.

This information stays on your phone. Nobody else can see it, including us.

Google sign-in and sync (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google to share records with your family, the app:

  • Uses your Google account's email address to show which account is signed in.
  • Saves a copy of your records in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the Drive "app data" folder). The app can only see this folder. It cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Reads that folder to bring in records added on the other phones in your household.

Your records go directly from your phone to Google, over an encrypted connection. They are stored under Google's own privacy policy. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

If you don't sign in, nothing leaves your phone.

Files you choose

When you import an Excel or CSV file, restore a backup, or export or share a file, the app opens only the file you pick. Sharing a file uses Android's share menu, and you choose where it goes.

Notifications

Reminders for bills and money due back are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent through any server.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal or financial information.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app doesn't read your SMS messages, contacts or location.
  • The app doesn't connect to your bank. Amounts are only what you type or import.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall Smart Hisab 360, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Smart Hisab 360 → Storage. This deletes everything the app stored on that phone.
  • In Google Drive: open drive.google.com on a computer → Settings (gear icon) → Manage apps → Smart Hisab 360 → Options → Delete hidden app data. You can also choose Disconnect from Drive. To remove the app's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because we never hold a copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Smart Hisab 360 is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy
